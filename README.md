## 👨‍💻 What I Do

- Extract, clean and transform retail data using Power Query
- Build reporting structures and management dashboards
- Analyze sales performance, margins, inventory and product rotation
- Monitor overstock, stockout risk and promotion impact
- Maintain weekly reporting used for operational decision-making
- Work with data supporting cross-market supply performance

---

## 🎯 Analytical Focus

I design reporting solutions that help commercial teams monitor KPIs, control profitability and make faster business decisions.

My main focus is not only dashboard creation, but also building reliable data foundations: validation, standardization, reconciliation of inconsistencies, missing data handling and scalable reporting structures.

Clean and structured data is the foundation of trustworthy analysis.
