## 📊 About Me

I work with retail sales, inventory and supply chain data, developing reporting solutions that support operational and commercial decision-making across multiple product departments.

My daily work combines business analysis, inventory management and data transformation. I focus on building clean, reliable and scalable reporting structures that help teams monitor performance, detect risks and make faster business decisions.

I am especially interested in the full analytical process — from raw data extraction and validation, through transformation and modeling, to final business insights and recommendations.

## 📂 Portfolio Direction

My portfolio focuses on practical business analytics projects based on realistic retail and commercial problems.

I build reports and dashboards that answer questions such as:

- Which products, categories or stores drive the result?
- Where is the risk of overstock or low rotation?
- How are sales, margin and stock changing over time?
- Which business areas require action?
- How can manual reporting be automated and standardized?
